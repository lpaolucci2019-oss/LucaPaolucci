The hierarchy — what actually matters, in order
1
Consistency is king
Showing up every day — even imperfectly — beats being perfect some days and gone the rest. Nothing else on this list matters if you quit.
L
I don't care if you hit your macros perfectly. I care that you're still here in week 12.
2
Discipline over motivation
Motivation is an emotion — it comes and goes. Discipline is a system. You won't always feel like tracking at 4am after a 12-hour shift. Do it anyway.
L
The nights you least want to do it are the nights that define whether this works.
3
Calories in, calories out
This is the only rule of fat loss. Not keto, not carnivore, not intermittent fasting. Science doesn't care about your feelings — a calorie deficit moves the scale.
L
I've seen this in critical care medicine. The science is settled. Trust it.
4
Protein first
Hit your protein target every single day before you worry about anything else. Protein keeps you full, preserves muscle, and makes everything easier on nights.
L
If you hit nothing else, hit your protein. The rest sorts itself out.
5
Sleep is non-negotiable
Poor sleep spikes cortisol, tanks your metabolism, and makes you crave junk. On nights especially, protecting your sleep is protecting your results.
L
You can't out-diet bad sleep. This is the most underrated part of the program.
6
Your schedule is your strategy
Stop following advice built for 9-to-5 people. Your shift IS your framework. We build everything around it — when you eat, train, and recover.
L
This is why I built this program. Nobody else is doing this for night shift nurses.
7
Progress over perfection
A bad day doesn't erase a good week. A bad week doesn't erase a good month. Stop starting over — just keep going from where you are.
L
The people who succeed aren't perfect. They're just the ones who didn't quit.
8
Trust the process
Six months feels like a long time until you're standing there down 30 pounds. The scale won't move every week. Stay the course.
L
Everyone who stuck with this looked back and said the same thing — I wish I started sooner.
L
I don't care if you hit your macros perfectly every day. I care that you're still here in week 12. Consistency is the whole game. Everything else is details.

Meal timing
Caffeine guide
Caffeine has a 5–6 hour half life. Timing matters more than amount.
General rules
200–400mg max per day · Wait 90 min after waking before first caffeine · Match every caffeinated drink with a glass of water · Limit energy drinks — high sugar and additives. Coffee, green tea, or low sugar options like Celsius are cleaner choices
Sleep protocol
On your days off
☀️
Get sunlight within 30 min of waking
Morning light anchors your rhythm. Even 10 minutes outside makes a measurable difference.
⏰
Wake at the same time every day off
Consistent wake time is the single most important factor for sleep quality on nights.
💊
Magnesium glycinate before sleep
200–400mg. Promotes deeper sleep, reduces cortisol.
Supplement guide
Vitamin D3 + K2
Most important
2,000–4,000 IU daily with food
Night shift nurses are chronically Vitamin D deficient — you sleep during peak sunlight hours. Linked to weight gain, fatigue, and immune dysfunction.
Magnesium Glycinate
Sleep quality
200–400mg before sleep

Weight loss pace
Slow and steady is the goal here. Night shift already puts your body under stress — we don't need to add a massive deficit on top of that. Pick a pace that feels sustainable.
Slow — recommended to start
Best for nights
−200 cal deficit · ~0.4 lbs per week · Easy to maintain, low stress on your body
Moderate
−350 cal deficit · ~0.7 lbs per week · Good balance of progress and sustainability
Aggressive
−500 cal deficit · ~1 lb per week · Only recommended once you are fully dialed in
Macro preference
Protein and total calories are what actually drive fat loss — those are locked in. The split between carbs and fat is personal preference. Pick whatever feels more sustainable for you.
Balanced
Recommended
Even split between carbs and fat. Works well for most people and easiest to maintain on nights.
Higher carbs
More carbs, less fat. Good if you prefer rice, pasta, fruit, and higher volume meals. Better for energy during long shifts.
Higher fat
More fat, less carbs. Good if you prefer eggs, avocado, nuts, and richer foods. Keeps you fuller longer between meals.
